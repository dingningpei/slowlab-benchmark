\paragraph{E1.} All three models ended below the baseline (Figure~\ref{fig:e1}, Table~\ref{tab:means}). DeepSeek: \res{E1.deepseek.mean}~\eur{} [\res{E1.deepseek.lo}, \res{E1.deepseek.hi}], $p_\text{Holm}$ \res{E1.deepseek.p}; MiMo: \res{E1.mimo.mean} [\res{E1.mimo.lo}, \res{E1.mimo.hi}], $p_\text{Holm}$ = \res{E1.mimo.p}; GLM: \res{E1.glm.mean} [\res{E1.glm.lo}, \res{E1.glm.hi}], $p_\text{Holm}$ = \res{E1.glm.p}, not significant, with a minimum detectable difference of \res{E1.glm.mde}~\eur{} at 80\% power, so a GLM effect of that size cannot be excluded either way.

The models did learn within the year: their final policies beat their pre-experiment recommendations by \res{learn.deepseek.mean} (DeepSeek, $p_\text{BH}$ = \res{learn.deepseek.p}), \res{learn.mimo.mean} (MiMo, \res{learn.mimo.p}) and \res{learn.glm.mean}~\eur{} (GLM, \res{learn.glm.p}). They ended near the fixed reference (\res{vsfixed.glm.mean}, \res{vsfixed.mimo.mean} and \res{vsfixed.deepseek.mean}~\eur{} for GLM, MiMo and DeepSeek), whereas the baseline exceeded it by \res{vsfixed.baseline.mean}~\eur{} [\res{vsfixed.baseline.lo}, \res{vsfixed.baseline.hi}], $p_\text{BH}$ \res{vsfixed.baseline.p}. The model policies used far less supplemental light than the baseline's (\res{res.glm.full.light}--\res{res.deepseek.full.light} against \res{res.baseline.full.light}~kWh/m$^2$ per crop) and more heat (\res{res.mimo.full.heat}--\res{res.deepseek.full.heat} against \res{res.baseline.full.heat}).

\paragraph{E2.} No method gained from in-season process feedback (Figure~\ref{fig:e2}): Full$-$Endpoint was \res{E2.deepseek.mean} [\res{E2.deepseek.lo}, \res{E2.deepseek.hi}] for DeepSeek, \res{E2.glm.mean} [\res{E2.glm.lo}, \res{E2.glm.hi}] for GLM, \res{E2.mimo.mean} [\res{E2.mimo.lo}, \res{E2.mimo.hi}] for MiMo and \res{E2.baseline.mean} [\res{E2.baseline.lo}, \res{E2.baseline.hi}]~\eur{} for the baseline; none is significant and every upper bound lies well below the 2~\eur{} effect of interest. The feedback gains did not differ between methods either (all $p_\text{BH} \ge$ \res{gaindiff.glm.baseline.p}).

The campaign records show why: no model ever stopped a running crop, the Full and Endpoint branches chose the same later planting schedule in \res{fb.deepseek.sameschedule}, \res{fb.glm.sameschedule} and \res{fb.mimo.sameschedule} of \res{fb.deepseek.campaigns} campaigns (DeepSeek, GLM, MiMo), and before the first later planting the Full branches read on average only \res{fb.deepseek.reads}--\res{fb.mimo.reads} channel windows and called the process predictor \res{fb.deepseek.predictor}--\res{fb.mimo.predictor} times. The opportunity to adapt existed in the interface but was rarely taken.

\paragraph{Cost and errors.} A branched campaign cost on average USD \res{cost.deepseek} (DeepSeek, \res{calls.deepseek} calls), \res{cost.glm} (GLM, \res{calls.glm}) and \res{cost.mimo} (MiMo, \res{calls.mimo}). MiMo produced markedly more format errors (\res{formaterr.mimo} per Full branch against \res{formaterr.deepseek} and \res{formaterr.glm}); these are part of the method's result.
