\section{Conclusion}
SlowLab tests whether an agent can run a year of slow, irreversible experiments in a shared facility and turn them into a good operating decision. Under a preregistered protocol, three low-cost language models improved on their first guesses (significantly only one of them) but stopped near the level of a standard grower setting, clearly below a prior-informed local optimiser, and did not exploit in-season process measurements; re-reading the final evidence did not change their recommendations significantly. We will release the benchmark, its locked protocol and the public records of the run so that stronger agents can be measured against the same sites.

\paragraph{Code and data availability.} The benchmark code, locked configurations, lock records and analysis outputs are available at \url{https://github.com/dingningpei/slowlab-benchmark} under the MIT licence. The public campaign records, evaluation records and history packets will be released as a data package under CC BY 4.0. The test-site and E3 sample seeds stay sealed (their salted commitments are public) so that the test sites remain unseen by future agents. The Cabauw weather data are provided by KNMI under CC BY 4.0 \citep{knmi_cabauw,knmi_cabauw_rad}.
