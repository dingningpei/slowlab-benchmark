\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
SlowLab benchmarks agents whose experiments are slow, irreversible and bound to four compartments, and judges them by one policy scored privately in unseen weather. On \res{sites} unseen sites, three low-cost language models stopped near a standard grower setting, clearly below a prior-informed local optimiser, and ran the facility as two synchronous batches that in-season measurements never changed. We release the benchmark and its locked protocol, and will release the run records, so that stronger agents can be measured on the same sites.

\paragraph{Code and data availability.} Code, locked configurations and analysis outputs: \url{https://github.com/dingningpei/slowlab-benchmark} (MIT licence). The campaign and evaluation records will be released under \ccby{}; the test-site and E3 seeds stay sealed (their salted commitments are public) so that the test sites remain unseen. Weather data: KNMI, \ccby{} \citep{knmi_cabauw,knmi_cabauw_rad}.
