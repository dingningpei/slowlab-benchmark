\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
SlowLab is a benchmark for agents that must learn from experiments that are slow, irreversible and bound to a small facility: each experiment occupies one of four compartments for half a year, its outcome arrives only at the end, and the agent is judged by a policy scored privately in weather it never saw. In a preregistered evaluation on \res{sites} unseen sites, three low-cost language models improved on their first guesses (significantly only one of them) but stopped near a standard grower setting, clearly below a prior-informed local optimiser; they ran the facility as two synchronous batches and did not profit from in-season measurements, and re-reading the final evidence did not change their recommendations significantly. We release the benchmark and its locked protocol, and will release the public records of the run, so that stronger agents can be measured against the same sites.

\paragraph{Code and data availability.} Code, locked configurations and analysis outputs: \url{https://github.com/dingningpei/slowlab-benchmark} (MIT licence). The campaign and evaluation records will be released under \ccby{}; the test-site and E3 seeds stay sealed (their salted commitments are public) so that the test sites remain unseen. Weather data: KNMI, \ccby{} \citep{knmi_cabauw,knmi_cabauw_rad}.
