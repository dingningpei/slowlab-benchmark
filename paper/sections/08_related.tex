\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
% Bibliography entries and characterisations of cited systems checked 2026-10-06 against arXiv, publisher and Crossref pages.
\paragraph{Agent benchmarks for experimentation.} BoxingGym gives language agents up to ten sequential experiments in each of ten generative probabilistic environments, scores experimental design by expected information gain, and scores model discovery by prediction error and by how well the agent's explanation lets a novice agent predict \citep{boxinggym}. CARE puts a language model inside a budgeted sequential reaction-optimisation loop: the model writes an executable scoring program, and a gate conditioned on a non-LLM reference policy decides whether the program's candidate replaces the reference choice; evaluation replays recorded campaigns offline \citep{care2026}. SlowLab differs in what an experiment costs: each one occupies a named unit for half a year, cannot be revised once started, and reveals its outcome only at the end, while process measurements accumulate in the meantime; the endpoint is an operating recommendation scored in new conditions.

\paragraph{Experiment-optimisation benchmarks and Bayesian optimisation.} Summit and Olympus provide executable reaction and materials benchmarks with noisy outcomes and planner libraries \citep{summit,olympus}; Minerva optimises large constrained parallel batches and benchmarks them on emulated reaction datasets \citep{minerva}. Budgeted Bayesian optimisation allocates a total evaluation-cost budget \citep{astudillo2021budgeted}; delayed-feedback BO chooses new evaluations while earlier ones are pending \citep{verma2022delayed}; trust-region BO restricts search to a region around the incumbent \citep{eriksson2019turbo}, which our baseline uses with a prior taken from a standard grower setting.

\paragraph{Scientific agents, self-driving laboratories and crop simulators.} Scientific-agent suites often start from existing data, while autonomous chemistry and materials systems connect planners to laboratory tools \citep{scienceagentbench,discoverybench,coscientist,chemcrow,szymanski2023autonomous,burger2020mobile}. Crop-simulator environments expose crop models as control problems \citep{wofostgym}; GreenLight models greenhouse climate and tomato growth under supplemental lighting \citep{katzin2020greenlight}. GreenLight-Gym builds a fast reinforcement-learning environment on GreenLight for learning greenhouse control policies under parametric uncertainty \citep{vanlaatum2025greenlightgym}; SlowLab instead evaluates how an agent designs, monitors and reads a year of irreversible experiments, with one recommended policy scored in new conditions. SlowLab is a simulated benchmark for asynchronous, facility-bound experimentation in one greenhouse domain; we do not infer validity in other domains. An earlier synchronous-protocol version of this benchmark \citep{slowlab_v21} is a pilot study only and enters none of the statistics here.
