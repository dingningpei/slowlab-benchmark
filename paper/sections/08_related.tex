\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
% Characterisations of cited systems follow the reviewed v2.1 related-work section; check before submission.
\paragraph{Agent benchmarks for experimentation.} BoxingGym gives language agents ten sequential experiments in generative probabilistic environments and evaluates experimental design by expected information gain, followed by prediction or model communication \citep{boxinggym}. CARE evaluates a language model inside a budgeted sequential reaction-optimisation loop, where the model writes a ranking program while a numerical reference policy retains control over experiment selection \citep{care2026}. SlowLab differs in what an experiment costs: each one occupies a named unit for half a year, cannot be revised once started, and reveals its outcome only at the end, while process measurements accumulate in the meantime; the endpoint is an operating recommendation scored in new conditions.

\paragraph{Experiment-optimisation benchmarks and Bayesian optimisation.} Summit, Olympus and Minerva provide executable reaction and materials benchmarks with noisy outcomes, planner libraries and, in Minerva, large constrained parallel batches \citep{summit,olympus,minerva}. Budgeted Bayesian optimisation allocates a total evaluation-cost budget \citep{astudillo2021budgeted}; delayed-feedback BO chooses new evaluations while earlier ones are pending \citep{verma2022delayed}; trust-region BO restricts search to a region around the incumbent \citep{eriksson2019turbo}, which our baseline uses with a prior taken from a standard grower setting.

\paragraph{Scientific agents, self-driving laboratories and crop simulators.} Scientific-agent suites often start from existing data, while autonomous chemistry and materials systems connect planners to laboratory tools \citep{scienceagentbench,discoverybench,coscientist,chemcrow,szymanski2023autonomous,burger2020mobile}. Crop-simulator environments expose crop models as control problems \citep{wofostgym}; GreenLight models greenhouse climate and tomato growth under supplemental lighting \citep{katzin2020greenlight}. GreenLight-Gym builds a fast reinforcement-learning environment on GreenLight for learning greenhouse control policies under parametric uncertainty \citep{vanlaatum2025greenlightgym}; SlowLab instead evaluates how an agent designs, monitors and reads a year of irreversible experiments, with one recommended policy scored in new conditions. SlowLab is a simulated benchmark for asynchronous, facility-bound experimentation in one greenhouse domain; we do not infer validity in other domains.
