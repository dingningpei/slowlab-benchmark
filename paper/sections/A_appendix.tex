\section{Baseline redesign}
\label{app:baseline}
The preregistered plan first named a plain asynchronous GP/BO as the only numerical comparator. In the eight-site pilot it scored \res{pilot.bo.vsfixed}~\eur{} relative to the fixed reference, which uses no experiments at all, and its two repeats on the same site differed with an SD of \res{pilot.bo.repeatsd}~\eur{} (language models: \res{pilot.llm.repeatsd.lo}--\res{pilot.llm.repeatsd.hi}). A comparator below the zero-experiment reference is not a meaningful baseline, so it was replaced before any test-site campaign was run; the decision referred only to the fixed reference. Its pilot and development results are reported here and it was not run on test sites.

The replacement differs in three ways, all set on development sites only. (i) It starts from the fixed reference and explores within a trust region around the best tried policy. (ii) Its Gaussian-process hyperparameters are frozen from development data (\res{kernel.groups} site-years, \res{kernel.crops} crops with random policies) instead of being refitted from two to six crops in each campaign. A kernel that separates a season effect common to all policies from the policy effect was chosen over a product kernel by a rule fixed before the fit: it ranked second-season crops from first-season data with accuracy \res{kernel.cross.interaction} against \res{kernel.cross.product}, and its held-out error on campaign-sized data was \res{kernel.rmse.interaction}~\eur{} against \res{kernel.rmse.refit} for per-campaign refitting and \res{kernel.rmse.mean} for the mean of the observed crops. (iii) Only tried policies are recommended. The initial trust-region radius was chosen among 15, 25 and 35\% of each range by mean development score (\res{radius.15.mean}, \res{radius.25.mean} and \res{radius.35.mean}~\eur{}; fixed reference \res{radius.fixed}; plain BO \res{radius.v3.mean}); the selected \res{radius.selected}\% is the smallest candidate, and the frozen rule allowed no further candidates. On the pilot sites its repeat SD was \res{pilot.baseline.repeatsd}~\eur{}.

\section{External validity check}
\label{app:external}
Before the study we tested whether GreenLight reproduces observed climate when driven by the actions of the 2019 Autonomous Greenhouse Challenge teams, in a one-shot temporal holdout with thresholds fixed in advance. It failed: pooled RMSE was \res{agc.temp.rmse}\,$^\circ$C for air temperature (threshold \res{agc.temp.limit}) and \res{agc.rh.rmse} percentage points for humidity (threshold \res{agc.rh.limit}); CO$_2$ passed (\res{agc.co2.rmse}~ppm, threshold \res{agc.co2.limit}). SlowLab is therefore a simulated benchmark; its numbers are not predictions for a real greenhouse.
