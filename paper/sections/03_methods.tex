\section{Methods compared}
\paragraph{Language-model agents.} Three low-cost models were run through their makers' own endpoints: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (official API, thinking disabled), MiMo V2.6 Flash (Xiaomi endpoint) and GLM-5.3 Flash (Z.AI endpoint; reasoning is mandatory there and capped at 1{,}024 tokens per call, a recorded asymmetry). Temperature 0.7 and at most 2{,}048 output tokens per call. Before any experiment each model gives a pre-experiment recommendation from the public task description only. The day-0 design is made once without knowing the feedback condition; the Endpoint branch replays it byte-identically and both branches then continue the same conversation under their assigned condition.

\paragraph{Main baseline: prior-informed local BO.} The numerical baseline uses the same interface, budget and tools. It starts from the frozen fixed reference policy (a standard grower setting chosen on development sites) in one compartment and orthogonal-design perturbations around it in another, adds two staggered compartments at mid-season (under Full feedback chosen from the public process predictor), and plants a second wave when the first crops finish. Its Gaussian process has a season-interaction kernel whose hyperparameters were fitted once on development data and frozen; expected improvement is maximised only inside a box trust region around the best tried policy \citep[TuRBO rules,][]{eriksson2019turbo}, with an initial radius of \res{radius.selected}\% of each range (the largest of three candidates) chosen on development sites by a rule fixed in advance; only tried policies are recommended. This baseline replaced a plain BO after the pilot showed the plain BO scoring below the fixed reference (Appendix~\ref{app:baseline}); the decision referred only to the fixed reference, never to language-model results.

\paragraph{References.} The \emph{fixed reference} applies the standard grower setting without experiments. A best-known reference from a numerical search on a random subset of sites bounds the headroom (Section~\ref{sec:e3}).
